# DuctForge changelog

What changed in DuctForge, newest first. Written from the history of its private source.

## September 2026

**22 Sep**
- Security update to the framework underneath the app.

**20 Sep**
- **Real printed pages.** The takeoff sheet is cut into A4 or Letter pages by rule, instead of one long sheet the printer chops wherever it likes.
- **Extras:** dampers, terminals, accessories and custom lines, counted on the takeoff and never added to the metal weight.
- Rebuilt for phones: one header row, sticky tabs and a compact takeoff list.
- Areas shown to four decimals, and the drawing scale no longer drifts with the precision setting.
- A saved PDF is named after the job.

**18 Sep**
- **Export studio.** Set the sheet up, preview it, and save it as PDF. Every part of the printed sheet can be switched on or off.
- **Flat pieces get their own group:** rectangular plate, circle, ring, plate with a round hole, frame, flat oval, triangle and trapezoid.

**9 to 10 Sep: first full review on real phones**
- A breakpoint of its own for phones, so dimension boxes stop clipping their values.
- Six wide tables now stack instead of scrolling sideways.
- An imperial takeoff reopens in inches, not in millimetres labelled "in".
- The square-to-round no longer draws its end view on top of its elevation.
- The drawing fills its frame, and opens full screen.
- Press Enter to add a line.
- Better contrast on secondary text.

## August 2026

**27 to 29 Aug**
- **One calculation, shown step by step,** with `=` meaning exact and `~` meaning rounded.
- A 3D view you can drag to turn.
- Exports get a letterhead, a shape and a name.
- Works offline once installed.
- A real logo and brand kit.
- The "dropper" is correctly called an Offset; flanges at both ends.
- Fraction input keeps full precision.

**24 Aug: first release**
- The takeoff engine, with an automated check over its arithmetic and drawing geometry.
- Every fitting drawn three ways: dimensioned blueprint, flat pattern with cut and fold lines, isometric.
- Two measuring standards: commercial billing and shop fabrication.
- Round duct, the other bill-of-quantity lines, and zones.
- A [standards page](https://ductforge.debugswift.com/standards) listing every formula and constant the calculator uses.
- A step-by-step guide in English, Bengali and Hindi.
